# FAQ

## How do we start?

Use the contact form on the website, or email hello@astibosoft.com. A short description of the problem or project is enough to begin a conversation.

## Do you only build websites?

No. Astibosoft also works on web applications and early product versions, not only marketing sites.

## Is there a fixed price list?

No public fixed price list. Fit and scope are discussed first so estimates match the real work.

## Can I book a call?

Use the contact form or email to request a conversation. Scheduling tools may be offered when useful.
